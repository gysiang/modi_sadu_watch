Exercise No. 1 Principles

Mouni Sadhu: 'Concentration'

The Core Object

Fix your visual and mental attention solely upon the extreme tip of the moving pointer (the luminous amber dot). Disregard the numbers, the casing, and even the concept of passing time. Nothing exists in the universe except that single luminous point in motion.

The Inflexible Rule of Intrusions

The moment any extraneous thought enters your consciousness—such as "I'm doing well", "How much time is left?", or an unrelated memory—the streak is instantly broken. Be scrupulously honest with yourself: tap the Thought Intrusion button immediately.

Eye Strain & Posture

Do not tense the facial muscles or strain the retinas. Blink naturally when required; blinking does not constitute a distraction so long as mental continuity on the point is preserved. Keep breathing rhythmic and calm.

The 10-Minute Benchmark

In esoteric mental training, achieving 10 uninterrupted minutes of perfect, continuous attention without a single thought intrusion indicates complete mastery over sensory perception and prepares the mind for the higher stages of meditation.

"Persistent, daily regular effort is of more value than sporadic hours of exertion."
